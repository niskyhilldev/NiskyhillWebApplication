Nisky Hill Cemetery

About Nisky Hill

Nisky Hill Cemetery is a historic burial ground in Bethlehem, Pennsylvania, established in the mid-19th century. Overlooking the Lehigh River, it is known for its scenic landscape and the graves of notable local figures, including industrialists, veterans, and Moravian community members. The cemetery reflects Bethlehem's rich history and serves as a peaceful resting place with well-preserved monuments and lush greenery.

About This Site

This site was built with the intent to inform and spread the history of Nisky Hill and the stories of the individuals who helped build that history.
